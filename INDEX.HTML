The elemental temples

The elemental temples stand as ancient guardians across the world, each perfectly placed to reflect the power it holds. From the blazing Fire Temple in lands of endless wildfires to the underwater chambers of the Water Temple off England’s coast, these temples blend into their extreme environments, making them almost impossible to reach without great skill or luck. High atop Mount Kilimanjaro, the Ice Temple shimmers like a frozen jewel, while deep in the Congo rainforest, the Plant Temple disappears beneath thick vines and towering trees.
Across deserts, mountains, and oceans, the temples hold not just elemental stones but also the history and ambition of the ancient Elemental Planet. In the storm-lit skies of Venezuela, the Electricity Temple hums with raw power, while the Earth Temple, carved into Mount Ethiopia, stands as a monument of strength. Some temples push the limits of reality itself — the Time Temple warps minutes and hours, the Space Temple floats on an asteroid, and the Gravity Temple, hidden under a space lab, twists the laws of nature.
In modern times, newer temples emerged, reflecting evolving forces like Speed, Magic, and Fear. The Speed Temple radiates energy so intense it quickens all life around it, while the Psychic Temple near Buddhist grounds heightens the mind’s power. Europe’s legendary Magic and Chaos Magic temples tie the past to the present, drawing on the mythic age of Merlin and King Arthur. Together, these temples form a hidden web across the planet, each fiercely protected and waiting for the brave to discover their secrets.
