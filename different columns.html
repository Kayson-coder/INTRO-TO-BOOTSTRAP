Long ago, on the Elemental Planet, an ancient civilization of powerful beings created the Elemental Stones to maintain balance, defense, and growth. Each stone harnessed a pure force of nature or cosmic energy — from fire and water to time, space, and chaos. To wield these powers, the civilization forged mighty weapons and forged golems, living guardians of stone, metal, energy, or magic, each bonded to a specific element. But over centuries, greed and ambition grew. Wars broke out as factions craved ultimate control over all the stones. Seeing the collapse of their world, the ancients made a desperate choice: they sealed the stones and weapons inside temples and sent them across the universe, away from corrupt hands. The stones landed on Earth, embedding themselves in landscapes that matched their nature — volcanoes, glaciers, jungles, caves, and even the depths of space. The temples became hidden behind natural hazards and magical barriers, while the golems awoke, programmed to destroy anyone unworthy. For centuries, humans remained unaware. But during the Golden Age of Exploration, explorers and adventurers stumbled upon these temples, leaving behind myths, legends, and fragmented maps. Rumors spread of lost treasures, strange phenomena, and deadly guardians lurking in places no one dared return to. Now, in modern times, the balance is crumbling again. Secret organizations, scientists, and rogue treasure hunters have begun locating the temples, hoping to harness their power. But disturbing the stones weakens the golems’ seals, and the ancient forces stir — threatening to unleash chaos upon the Earth. A new generation of heroes must rise, each drawn to a specific element, to face the challenges, earn the stones’ trust, and stop the return of the ancient elemental war before Earth becomes the new battlefield.
Long ago, on the mysterious Elemental Planet, a mighty civilization thrived. To defend their world and advance their society, they forged the Elemental Stones — primal forces bound in crystal, each linked to a natural or cosmic element: Fire, Electricity, Earth, Air, Water, Plant, Ice, Crystal, Darkness, Light, Time, Space, Technology (born from alien knowledge), Venom, Lava, Bone, Devil, and Gravity. With these stones, they crafted weapons of immense power, and to guard them, they built golems — living titans molded from stone, metal, energy, or magic, each perfectly tuned to its element. At first, the stones were used wisely, but as generations passed, desire and ambition took root. Factions arose, each craving dominance over all stones to reshape the world. Endless wars erupted. Seeing their world on the brink of collapse, the ancients made a desperate decision: the stones, weapons, and golems were locked inside colossal temples, and the entire network was cast across space — to Earth, a young, evolving world. The temples arrived hidden in Earth’s most extreme, remote, or magical places: The Fire Temple settled in a desert of daily wildfires. The Electricity Temple struck roots in Venezuela’s Catatumbo storm. The Earth Temple embedded itself in Mount Ethiopia’s rugged cliffs. The Air Temple landed in the USA’s tornado-prone prairies. The Water Temple sank into an underwater cave near England. The Plant Temple hid in the tangled depths of the Congo rainforest. The Ice Temple froze atop Mount Kilimanjaro. The Crystal Temple nestled in a glowing Mexican crystal cave. The Darkness Temple was buried in a pitch-black U.S. cave. The Light Temple radiated in China’s Yarlung Valley. The Time Temple warped a remote island where time fractured. The Space Temple rested on a massive asteroid near the moon. The Technology Temple hid under Asia’s most advanced airport. The Venom Temple spread its toxins in the Amazon rainforest. The Lava Temple roared beside a Hawaiian volcano. The Bone Temple rose among dinosaur fossils in the Gobi Desert. The Devil Temple emerged in Australia’s burning outback. The Gravity Temple buried itself under a space lab in the USA. In modern times, new temples and stones awakened: Animal (Yellowstone), Psychic (China), Speed (a secret valley), Magic and Chaos Magic (Europe’s Merlin legends), Energy, Sound, Fear, Combat, Ninja, Shadow, Power, Metal, Astral, and Psychic stones. Each temple was protected by an elemental golem, matching the environment and able to unleash its full fury on intruders. Across centuries, the temples were hidden, becoming legends — but in the Golden Age of Exploration, humans began finding them. Explorers disappeared; adventurers whispered of cursed treasures and impossible guardians. Yet none could break the seals or claim the stones. Now, in modern times, as technology and science pierce even Earth’s last mysteries, secret organizations, governments, and criminals have begun seeking these stones again. The disturbance is awakening the golems — and worse, the elemental balance is breaking. Earth trembles on the brink of disaster as the ancient elemental war threatens to ignite once more. But hope remains. Across the globe, a new generation of elemental guardians is awakening — ordinary people chosen by the stones themselves. They must face the ancient golems, master their elemental powers, and stop the forces racing to awaken the final, most dangerous stones: Chaos Magic, Devil, and Gravity. Only together can they prevent Earth from becoming the battlefield where the Elemental Planet’s forgotten war finishes… and possibly destroys all life.
